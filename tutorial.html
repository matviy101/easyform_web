How to use EasyForm

Find the right information for your gym equipment in just a few simple steps.

1

Find the QR code

Look for an EasyForm QR code attached to or near the gym equipment.

2

Scan the QR code

Use your phone's camera to scan the QR code and open the EasyForm page.

3

Learn about the equipment

Explore the exercises, equipment information, and form tips for that muscle group.
